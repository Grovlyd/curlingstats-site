From the first stone to the team review

Record

Every shot in a few taps

Place the broom and the target, then mark where the stone stopped. The app works out the shot type and the turn and suggests a score. You can correct it at any time.

  • Classic curling and mixed doubles
  • Score from 0 to 4 by distance to the target
  • A flag and a comment on any shot
Replay

Walk through any end again

Stones move and collide on screen, shot by shot. Next to each one you see who threw it, the score and what you flagged during the game.

  • Any end of any game
  • Miss reasons next to the shot
Statistics

See where the team wins points and where it loses them

Percentages by player, position and shot type, per end and per game. The map shows where stones stopped and how accurate each shot was.

  • Team against team
  • Player against player

How to get Premium

  1. Install the app and sign up with your email and a password. A confirmation code will arrive by email.
  2. Open “Premium”, choose a period and tap the button to continue.
  3. Pay on the YooKassa page and return to the app. Premium turns on automatically.

One-time payment for the chosen period, with no automatic renewal. Prices are in Russian rubles, the payment is processed by YooKassa, and the terms are in Russian: public offer.

Delivery and refunds

This is a digital service, nothing is shipped. Premium turns on automatically in your account on every device where you are signed in, usually within a few minutes after payment. If it does not, write to curlingstats@yandex.ru with your account email and the payment date.

Refunds. You can cancel at any time. We refund the unused part of the paid period within 10 days to the card or account used for payment. Full terms are in the public offer (in Russian).
